A CNN trained in Keras on FER-2013 classifies facial emotions, while an OpenCV and Tkinter pipeline detects faces in live video and overlays matching emojis. Tuned with dropout, data augmentation, and the Adam optimizer.

**Stack:** Keras, OpenCV, CNN, Tkinter

Code: [github.com/RanaNameer](https://github.com/RanaNameer)
